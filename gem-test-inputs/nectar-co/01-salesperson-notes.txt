Project: Nectar & Co. Wellness Cafe
Customer contact: Maya Patel
Location: Endcap retail space in new shopping center
Stage: Customer liked the mockup and wants us to move toward final design/pricing

What customer responded well to:
- Bright, fresh, upscale health/wellness look
- Circular fruit-style logo at left
- Main name large and readable above canopy
- "Wellness Cafe" smaller underneath
- Color combo of warm orange/coral + teal
- Window graphics with fruit imagery and a clean, premium feel
- Likes that the storefront feels bold but still clean

What I think is approved:
- Overall look and feel
- Main sign above storefront
- Window graphics across front and side windows
- Black awning/canopy stays

What is probably not final:
- Exact logo artwork
- Exact font
- Exact colors
- Exact wording on windows
- Exact scale/proportions
- Fruit images are probably placeholders
- Side window text may just be conceptual

Customer comments from meeting:
- "This is the first version that feels like us."
- "I like the fruit icon and the colors."
- "I want it to feel premium, not too playful."
- "Please keep it clean and not cluttered."
- "I want people driving by to understand immediately that we do juices, tea, and wellness."
- "I don't want it looking too much like a smoothie shop for teenagers."

Open items I do not have yet:
- Final logo files
- Brand standards
- Exact copy for window messaging
- Landlord criteria package
- Exact storefront dimensions
- Whether main sign should be illuminated
- Whether windows are perforated vinyl or standard vinyl
